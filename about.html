About Gupta Dental Clinic

Recognized by GDCH Mumbai for excellence in clinical dentistry, Gupta Dental Clinic – Aesthetic Smile & Implant Centre is Giridih’s premier destination for high-end Orthodontics, Smile Designing, and advanced Implantology. Led by Dr. Pratikshit Anand, we combine elite institutional standards with AI-enhanced technology to provide painless, precision-driven dental care. We specialize in discreet Braces, Clear Aligners, and custom Digital Smile Makeovers that restore both function and confidence. Using 3D-guided technology for Dental Implants and Zirconia Crowns, we ensure world-class, aesthetic, and long-lasting results. We are dedicated to providing a premium experience for every patient.
