Preventive care

Understanding routine dental visits

General educational information; individual recommendations require a dental examination.

A visit begins with your concerns

Bring up changes you have noticed, including pain, sensitivity, bleeding gums, difficulty chewing or previous dental work. A dentist can examine the teeth and gums and discuss whether diagnostic imaging is appropriate.

Questions to ask

  • What did the examination find?
  • What options are available, and what are their likely stages?
  • How should I care for my teeth and gums at home?
  • Which symptoms should prompt another visit?
